# 17 — Trust, Safety, and Child-Conscious Platform Policy

**Status:** Policy proposal; requires jurisdiction-specific legal review before public launch.  
**Launch position:** All-ages environment; adult/sexual content not supported.

## 1. Policy goals

Create a comfortable environment for children and general audiences while allowing legitimate fictional game genres such as shooters, fighting games, survival, fantasy combat and moderate horror. Safety is a product and operations requirement, not just a filter.

## 2. Sexual content policy

Sexualized and sexually explicit content is prohibited across all age labels and product surfaces: games, assets, animations, emotes, UI, thumbnails, profile items, MUTE libraries, advertisements and user-generated content. This includes sexualized presentation of fictional characters and any sexualization or exploitation of minors. Ordinary nonsexual affection/friendship is not automatically sexual content. Suspected child sexual abuse/exploitation material is handled by designated trained personnel under restricted incident procedures; ordinary moderators must not download, copy or circulate such material.

## 3. Violence policy

Fictional violence is not automatically prohibited. Moderators assess context, realism, intensity, duration, gore, suffering, rewards/encouragement, audience and presentation. Stylized combat, fantasy weapons, non-graphic deaths, FPS gameplay and limited stylized blood/gore may be allowed with suitable labels. Prolonged torture, graphic realistic mutilation, real-world injury footage, extreme cruelty or content celebrating real-world harm is prohibited or escalated under detailed policy.

Proposed Poko labels: `Poko E`, `Poko 10+`, `Poko 13+`. These are internal platform labels, not formal ESRB/PEGI classifications. They must be validated by moderation policy and market/legal review. No 18+ category at launch.

## 4. Submission moderation

Every newly uploaded public asset receives human review before distribution. Every public game release receives human review before distribution. Technical scans assist triage but never auto-approve. Reviews cover all assets, scripts, audio, characters, animations, UI, gameplay, user-generated interactions and dependencies. Immutable hash-bound approvals are mandatory.

## 5. Player communication MVP

Recommended initial defaults:

- Quick Chat with approved phrase list.
- Block and mute.
- Reporting for player/game/asset/library/message/server.
- No voice chat at launch.
- No unrestricted private messaging at launch.
- No unrestricted user image/audio upload in games at launch.
- External links blocked or tightly controlled by platform policy.
- If text chat is added, it must use platform moderation, rate limits, anti-spam and personal-information protections.

A profanity list alone does not address grooming, manipulation, harassment or attempts to move children off-platform.

## 6. Privacy defaults

Collect minimum data required. Do not expose email, credentials, IP addresses, precise location or internal authentication IDs to creators/players. Use game-scoped pseudonymous identifiers for creator APIs where practical. Limit retention of chat/report/telemetry data, document access, and use age-appropriate defaults. Do not use invasive age verification unless a specific legal/product requirement justifies it and privacy review is complete.

## 7. Reports

Reports go directly to PokoX independent of creator servers. Categories include sexual content/exploitation, inappropriate content, harassment, grooming/solicitation, personal information/doxxing, cheating, scams, dangerous content, copyright and other. A report creates a case, not an automatic ban. Prioritize suspected exploitation/urgent safety cases.

## 8. Moderator staffing and workload

Human review capacity is limited. Use creator submission quotas, queue transparency, backlog pausing and priority escalation. Never auto-approve due to queue pressure. Provide policy training, content warnings, limited exposure, escalation access and staff support. Do not require ordinary volunteer moderators to inspect illegal material directly.

## 9. Enforcement ladder

Possible actions: content rejection, request changes, remove content, disable public discovery/download, suspend game, restrict publishing, temporary account restriction, account suspension/termination, server credential revocation, and escalation to appropriate authorities where legally required. Apply proportionality and evidence requirements. Severe child-safety matters follow a dedicated incident/legal process.

## 10. Parent/guardian controls

Potential controls: chat setting, friend/invite permissions, privacy visibility, time reminders, purchase history visibility and account restrictions. A parental PIN is not proof of age or guardian identity. Features must be tested and legally reviewed before representing them as parental controls.

## 11. Dynamic user-generated content

Player-written signs/names/descriptions require filtering, rate limits and reporting. Initially prohibit unrestricted player-uploaded images/audio during live gameplay. Games that permit user-generated structures/text must declare this at review and provide moderation controls. Approved game packages cannot bypass platform safety rules by generating content dynamically.

## 12. Age labels and discovery

Filter content suitability before ranking. Show content labels and descriptors on game page and search. Do not optimize solely for time-on-platform. Use curated categories and safety-aware ranking; avoid behavioral profiling that may amplify harmful content.

## 13. Sensitive incident handling

Restrict access, preserve only required evidence under controlled policy, prevent staff from copying or circulating illegal content, record chain of custody where required, and escalate to trained personnel/legal authorities according to applicable law. Establish written procedures with Philippine counsel and additional market counsel before operating in other jurisdictions.

## 14. Legal and policy references

Review Philippine RA 11930, the Data Privacy Act and National Privacy Commission guidance, plus child online safety requirements in every launch jurisdiction. This document is not legal advice and must not be treated as a complete compliance assessment. See `REFERENCES.md` for starting points.

## 15. Final violence/content-label interpretation

PokoX is an all-ages platform and does not offer an 18+ category at launch. Sexualized or sexually explicit content is prohibited across games, characters, animations, assets, profiles, thumbnails, audio, libraries and player interactions; it is not unlocked for adult accounts.

Fictional violence is not automatically prohibited. Combat, fictional weapons, character deaths, first-person shooters, military-style fictional gameplay, monsters, horror and bounded stylized blood/gore may be permitted according to context, presentation, intensity and the assigned PokoX content label. Reviewers distinguish ordinary game combat from prolonged graphic mutilation, realistic injury footage, torture, cruelty-focused content or content that glorifies real-world harm. Such extreme depictions are rejected. The final labels `Poko E`, `Poko 10+`, and `Poko 13+` are PokoX internal labels, not official ESRB/PEGI classifications; display them as PokoX labels only.

Moderation is human-led for all new public assets and releases. Automated checks can validate technical structure and prioritize risk, but must not auto-approve public content. A title's violence label and all other content descriptors are confirmed by moderators. Re-review changed releases; preserve exact hash-bound approval only for unchanged approved dependencies where the current policy version allows it.

## 16. Launch communication defaults

At initial public launch: no voice chat, unrestricted direct messages, player-uploaded in-game images/audio or arbitrary external links. Quick Chat uses curated messages. Text chat, if enabled in a later phase, must use server-side filtering, rate limits, personal-information controls, reporting and age-appropriate defaults. Players can block, mute and report. Reports go directly to PokoX and cannot be suppressed by creator servers.

## 17. Review operations and staff safety

Use least-privilege moderator roles, sensitive-content escalation, staff audit, evidence access logs and exposure minimization. Suspected child sexual abuse/exploitation material is handled only under a documented legal incident process by specifically authorized personnel. Ordinary reviewers must not download, copy, forward or circulate suspected illegal material. Obtain local legal review before public launch for each launch jurisdiction.
